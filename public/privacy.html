Privacy notice

Effective 27 November 2026 · Kivotech Limited (RC 8360347)

This notice explains what personal data Kivotech Limited (“Kivo”, “we”) collects through kivo.africa, why, who we share it with and the rights you have under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the NDPA General Application and Implementation Directive 2025. It covers this website, the early access list and demo bookings. The Kivo product has its own privacy terms, shared with customers before they sign.

1. Who we are

Kivotech Limited is the data controller for this website. Registered in Nigeria, RC 8360347. Registered office: Lagos, Nigeria. For anything about your data, email hello@kivo.africa with “Privacy request” in the subject.

2. What we collect

  • Early access form: your name, work email, company or estate, role, the kind of buildings you look after, number of sites, what you’re interested in and, if you choose, your WhatsApp number.
  • Demo bookings: the details you give on our Zoho Bookings page, such as name, email, phone and preferred time.
  • Messages: what you send us by email or WhatsApp.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider keeps short-lived server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We don’t use these logs to profile you.

We don’t collect sensitive personal data through this website, and we don’t use cookies or tracking tools. See our cookie notice.

3. Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To respond to your request for early access or a demo, set up your workspace and arrange founding member pricing. Lawful basis: steps you asked us to take before a contract, and our legitimate interest in replying to people who contact us.
  • To send product updates and launch news. Lawful basis: your consent, given by ticking the box on the form. You can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link in any email or by writing to us.
  • To keep the website secure and working. Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in running a safe service.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as tax, accounting or a lawful request from a regulator.

We don’t sell personal data, and we don’t make decisions about you by automated means alone.

4. Who we share it with

Only with service providers who process it on our instructions:

  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France) stores early access contacts and sends our emails.
  • Vercel Inc. (United States) hosts this website and processes form submissions.
  • Zoho Corporation provides our business email and demo bookings.
  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) carries any messages you choose to send us there.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone’s safety.

5. Transfers outside Nigeria

Some of these providers store or process data outside Nigeria, including in the European Union and the United States. Where we transfer personal data abroad we rely on the transfer bases in Part VIII of the NDPA, such as the adequacy of the destination’s data protection framework and appropriate safeguards in our contracts with these providers.

6. How long we keep it

We keep early access and demo details for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a customer, in which case our customer terms apply. If you ask us to delete your data sooner, we will, unless the law requires us to keep it. Server logs are kept by our host for a short period only.

7. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your data;
  • restrict or stop how we use it, including objecting to direct marketing at any time;
  • give you your data in a commonly used, machine-readable format, or send it to another organisation;
  • withdraw consent, without affecting what we did before you withdrew it.

Email hello@kivo.africa. We’ll confirm we’ve received your request and respond within the time the NDPA allows. You may also send us a Standard Notice to Address Grievance, and you have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), limit access to people who need it, and choose providers with recognised security practices. No system is completely secure; if a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will tell you and the Commission as the NDPA requires.

9. Children

This website is for businesses and their staff. It is not directed at children, and we don’t knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this notice

If we change how we use personal data, we’ll update this page and the effective date above, and tell early access members about significant changes.

When does Kivo launch?+

Kivo is launching on 1 January 2027. Early access members are first in line. The team is currently piloting with facility teams in Lagos.

What does early access cost?+

Nothing. Joining is free, with no payment required today. Founding members receive 20% off their first six months when Kivo launches, plus free Excel data setup.

Do residents or staff need a paid account?+

No. Residents, staff and occupants can log and track requests free. Estate plans start from a per-unit rate; commercial plans charge for the users who manage the work.

We use Excel and WhatsApp. Is switching difficult?+

Founding members get free setup. Share your existing sites, assets and contractors in Excel and Kivo’s team will import them. You’ll have a direct WhatsApp line to the founders for support.

Can we manage more than one site?+

Yes. Kivo organises sites, buildings and floors in one hierarchy, with tickets, assets and contractors connected to their locations. FM firms and larger portfolios can discuss enterprise pricing with the team.

Does Kivo connect to our accounting software?+

Integrations with Sage, QuickBooks, Xero and Microsoft Dynamics are on the 2027 roadmap. They are not part of the initial launch.

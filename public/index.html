      When does Kivo launch, and what’s in it?+

      Kivo FM launches on 1 January 2027 with everything on this page: requests and tickets, technicians’ jobs on mobile, contractor and vendor links, stores and diesel, LPOs and waybills, supplier bill matching, client billing and reports. Kivo for construction follows later.

      What does early access cost?+

      Nothing. Joining is free. Business, Estates and Hotels customers who sign by 31 March 2027 get 20% off their first six months and free setup from your Excel sheets. Multi-site and FM Partner customers start with a 60-day pilot at half price.

      Who counts as a paid user?+

      On the Business plan, the people who do and manage the work: admins, technicians and storekeepers. Staff, residents and occupants who only report issues are always free. Other plans count units, rooms, sites or technicians instead.

      Do contractors need to download Kivo?+

      No. Each contractor or vendor gets the job or LPO by email, with a link to a page for just that job. Replying to the email adds a note to the job. Urgent jobs can also go out on WhatsApp in one tap.

      Whose name is on the emails?+

      Yours. Emails show your organisation’s name, such as “Meridian Bank Facilities”, and replies go to the address you choose. Sending from your own domain is optional, a one-time setup with your IT team, included on every plan.

      How do you handle VAT and withholding tax?+

      VAT at 7.5% is added to Kivo invoices and to the client bills you send from Kivo. When a client deducts 5% withholding tax, the bill is still marked paid and Kivo keeps track of the credit note you’re owed.

      We use Excel and WhatsApp. Is switching difficult?+

      Send us your sites, assets, stock and contractors in Excel and Kivo’s team imports them. Founding members get this free, and a direct WhatsApp line to the founders.

      Does Kivo connect to our accounting software?+

      Connections to Sage, QuickBooks, Xero and Microsoft Dynamics are coming soon. They show in settings at launch but don’t sync yet.
